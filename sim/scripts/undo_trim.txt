# Trim, overdub, trim undo. An 18 s loop trimmed to 4 s: an overdub's undo
# cells go at the end of the buffer (21 s less 4 s), over the trimmed-away
# part of the loop. Undoing the trim has to bring that part back as it was,
# not with the cells in it (the cells hold the kept part: another pitch).
wait 300
tone_shape steady
# 18 s (9 bars at 120 bpm; a count-in first): 330 Hz, then 550 Hz from 10 s.
tone 330
tap 0
wait 12000
tone 550
wait 7500
tap 0
wait 1000
tone off
expect_state 1 PLAY
expect_len 1 18.0
# Keep the first 4 s, then replace a pass of it with 880 Hz.
press INSTR
press F2
enc -28
press F4
wait 100
press NOTE
expect_len 1 4.0
tone 880
hold SHIFT
press F1
release SHIFT
wait 4500
press F1
tone off
wait 500
# The trim undone: 18 s again, 17.5 s .. 18 s (under the cells) still 550 Hz.
press INSTR
press F7
wait 100
press NOTE
expect_len 1 18.0
press PLAY
wait 500
press PLAY
wait 17550
reset_levels
wait 400
expect_pitch 550
