# Copy on the home page with a long take (its tail on the card): the copy is
# a file job, read as the take plays, and goes into the next track in time
# with it, its own tail on its own stream file. A 30 s take: 330 Hz for 15 s,
# then 660 Hz (from 19.5 s on, on the card).
wait 300
opt all start 0
tone_shape steady
tone 330
tap 0
wait 14980
tone 660
wait 15000
tap 0
tone off
wait 50
expect_stream 1 long 1
expect_len 1 30.0

press COPY
wait 4000
expect_state 2 PLAY
expect_len 2 30.0
expect_stream 2 long 1
shot ../build/sim/check/dup_long.png
