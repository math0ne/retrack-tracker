# The mixer: holding Shift, F1..F7 mute and unmute the strips (the tracks,
# the two FX returns, master; the strip picked stays); a muted strip says so.
wait 300
opt all start 0
tone_shape steady
tone 330
tap 0
wait 2000
tap 0
wait 300
tone off
expect_state 1 PLAY
press MASTER
wait 50
hold SHIFT
wait 50
shot ../build/sim/check/mixer_shift.png
press F1
wait 50
release SHIFT

wait 500
expect_silent

press F1

wait 500
