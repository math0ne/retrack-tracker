# The sound board plays any PCM WAV: 24-bit at 48 kHz, 16-bit at 96 kHz
# (WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE), 8-bit at 22.05 kHz, 32-bit float, each at its
# own pitch (resampled to 44.1 kHz). A file it cannot play says why. The
# sample loader previews a WAV (F3); the mixer has the board's strip (Right
# from master): its level, DJ filter and sends, kept with the board. The
# monitored input runs through the selected track's DJ filter.
wait 300
tone off
sd_wav 0wavs/a24_48k.wav 1.5 48000 24 2
sd_wav 0wavs/b16_96k.wav 1.5 96000 16 2 ext
sd_wav 0wavs/c8_22k.wav 1.5 22050 8 1
sd_wav 0wavs/d32f.wav 1.5 44100 32 1 float
sd_file 0wavs/e_bad.wav 100
